[assistant] The reviewer's comment asked to keep the query runner free of other products, move the flag lookup into `backend/logic`, and read flags from event properties for v1. I've updated the plan to do that (now at revision 5) and replied to the comment.

- **Removed:** the query runner no longer depends on the feature flags product directly.
